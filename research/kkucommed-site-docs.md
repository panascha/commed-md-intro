## Summary

The KKU Community Medicine department website (https://kkucommed.kku.ac.th/) is a WordPress site publicly accessible — no login required. The crawler visited the homepage, top menu pages (about, curriculum, field training, departments, student life, contact), category archives, paginated post listings, and linked pages across KKU subdomains (registrar, law division, research, GS, main university site).

**Key finding:** The site contains predominantly two types of downloadable documents:
1. **COMMED-specific learning materials and field-training documents** (highly relevant for COMMED 2569 course)
2. **KKU-wide administrative documents** from linked subdomains (mostly calendars, strategic plans, orders) — low relevance to COMMED coursework

No login wall was encountered; all documents were downloaded successfully via standard HTTP with browser User-Agent headers.
